What the agent does differently
Auto-approve routine cases
60–70% of routine requests meet criteria automatically. The agent approves them in <2 minutes — no human touch required.
Context-rich routing — not just a flag
Complex cases go to reviewers with policy text, clinical criteria, member history, and a structured reasoning summary — cutting review time from 45 min to 8 min.
Every decision is auditable
Full trace of which policy section was cited, which criteria were met, and which data points drove the decision — CMS-audit-ready by design.
Human in the loop for denials
The agent never auto-denies. All denials require clinician sign-off with documented reasoning — protecting both compliance and care quality.
