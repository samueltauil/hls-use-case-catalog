Sales Discovery

The questions that open the deal

Lead with the CFO's number. Every question anchors to financial impact.

Q1
"What is your current initial denial rate — and what does it cost your team to rework a single denied claim?"
→ Establishes the baseline. Industry avg is ~15% initial denial rate / ~$57 per rework (HFMA/Premier 2025). Multiply by volume and the number becomes undeniable.
Q2
"Which payers and denial reason codes are driving the most loss — and do you know that answer today without pulling a report?"
→ Most teams don't. Fabric analytics positions as the visibility they're missing — surfaces systemic patterns vs. one-off rework.
Q3
"How far upstream can you intervene today — and does your pre-bill review team have the data they need to catch at-risk claims?"
→ Exposes whether the workflow exists. If pre-bill review is manual and gut-feel based, AI scoring is the missing layer.
Q4
"What would a 3–4 point reduction in your denial rate be worth annually — and is that a number your CFO has on their dashboard?"
→ Anchors the ROI. For most systems it's $3–7M annually. Once the CFO hears that number, the conversation changes.
